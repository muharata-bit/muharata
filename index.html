OUR JOURNEY

Four decades of building forward.

A simplified timeline based on the company's published history.

1983

The beginning

Muharata began with a single posho mill in Kariobangi and grew into agro-processing machinery fabrication.

1998

New product lines

The company widened its range to include maize hullers, silos and storage-related equipment.

2000s

Regional expansion

The customer base expanded beyond Kenya while new machinery lines included oil presses, seed threshers, peanut and root/tuber processing.

Today

Engineering for tomorrow

Muharata continues to engage farmers and processors through trade fairs and local shows while expanding its machinery solutions.
